NoteSaver

Your notes, organized and backed up.

NoteSaver is an Android note-taking app that helps you create and manage your notes.

Features

  • Create and manage personal notes.
  • Keep notes and backups stored locally on your device.
  • Use Google Drive backup and restore when available and authorized.
