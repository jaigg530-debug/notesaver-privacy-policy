# NoteSaver Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 25, 2026 NoteSaver (“the app”) provides a way to create and manage notes and back them up to Google Drive. This policy explains how the app handles information. ## 1. Information We Handle NoteSaver may store notes and related information that you enter into the app. The app may also create backup files containing your notes when you use its backup feature. ## 2. Local Storage Your notes and local backups may be stored on your device. The app does not require you to upload your notes to a remote server to use its basic note-taking features. ## 3. Google Drive Backups If you choose to use Google Drive backup or restore, NoteSaver requests permission to access the specific Google Drive files created or selected for use with the app. With your authorization, the app can create or upload backup files to Google Drive and retrieve those files when you restore a backup. Google Drive access is provided through Google's services and is subject to Google's privacy policy and terms. ## 4. Google Account Information When you connect a Google account, Google may share information needed to authenticate your account and authorize Drive access. NoteSaver uses this authorization to provide its Google Drive backup and restore features. NoteSaver does not ask for your Google account password. ## 5. Sharing and Disclosure NoteSaver does not sell your personal information. Your notes and backup contents are not intentionally shared by NoteSaver with third parties, except as needed to provide features you request, comply with applicable law, or protect rights and security. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion You can manage or delete notes within the app, subject to the app's available features. You can also manage backup files in Google Drive through your Google account. Deleting a local note or uninstalling the app may not delete backup files already stored in Google Drive. You may need to delete those files separately. ## 7. Security NoteSaver uses your device's storage and Google's authorized services to provide its features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 8. Children's Privacy NoteSaver is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when NoteSaver's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. ## 10.
