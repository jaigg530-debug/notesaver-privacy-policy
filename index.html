NoteSaver Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

NoteSaver provides a way to create and manage notes and back them up to Google Drive. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Handle

NoteSaver may store the notes and related information you enter into the app. When you use backup features, the app may create backup files containing your notes.

2. Local Storage

Your notes and local backups may be stored on your device. Basic note-taking does not require uploading your notes to a remote server.

3. Google Drive Backups

If you choose to use Google Drive backup or restore, NoteSaver will request authorization to access the Google Drive files necessary for that feature. Depending on the permissions granted and the implementation, the app may upload backup files to Google Drive and retrieve them when you restore a backup.

4. Google Account Information

Google sign-in and Drive access are handled through Google's authentication services. NoteSaver does not ask you to provide your Google account password directly to the app. Google services are subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

NoteSaver does not sell your personal information. Your notes and backup contents are not intentionally shared by NoteSaver with third parties, except as necessary to provide features you request, comply with applicable law, or protect rights and security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage or delete notes within the app, subject to its available features. Backup files stored locally or in Google Drive may need to be deleted separately. Uninstalling the app may not delete backup files already stored in Google Drive.

7. Security

NoteSaver uses device storage and authorized services to provide its features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

NoteSaver is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when NoteSaver's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
