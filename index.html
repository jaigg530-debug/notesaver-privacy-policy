NoteSaver Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

NoteSaver provides a way to create and manage notes and back them up to Google Drive. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Handle

NoteSaver may store notes and related information that you enter into the app. The app may also create backup files containing your notes when you use its backup feature.

2. Local Storage

Your notes and local backups may be stored on your device. Basic note-taking does not require uploading notes to a remote server.

3. Google Drive Backups

If you choose Google Drive backup or restore, NoteSaver requests permission to access the specific Google Drive files created or selected for use with the app. With your authorization, the app can upload backup files and retrieve them when you restore a backup.

Google Drive access is provided through Google's services and is subject to Google's privacy policy and terms.

4. Google Account Information

Google may share information needed to authenticate your account and authorize Drive access. NoteSaver uses this authorization to provide its backup and restore features. NoteSaver does not ask for your Google account password.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

NoteSaver does not sell your personal information. Notes and backup contents are not intentionally shared by NoteSaver with third parties, except as needed to provide requested features, comply with applicable law, or protect rights and security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage or delete notes within the app, subject to its available features. You can also manage backup files in Google Drive. Deleting a local note or uninstalling the app may not delete backups already stored in Google Drive.

7. Security

NoteSaver uses your device's storage and Google's authorized services to provide its features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

NoteSaver is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when NoteSaver's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
